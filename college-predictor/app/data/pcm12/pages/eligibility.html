How the percentages are counted
PCM %(Physics + Chemistry + Maths totals) ÷ 300. Totals include practical or internal marks.
5-subject %(PCM + English + one more subject) ÷ 500. Used by JoSAA.
TNEA cut-offMaths + Physics ÷ 2 + Chemistry ÷ 2, out of 200.

Step 3 · If the gap is too big

Other routes and what to check first.

No route is a guaranteed fallback. Each one has its own rules, fees and seats, so check the current notice and speak to a counsellor.

Reported 2026

Improve one subject

In 2026, CBSE let Class 12 students choose one subject for improvement in the July supplementary exam. Before you rely on it, check whether JoSAA, DASA or the university counts improved marks.

Third-party

State counselling with lower cut-offs

TNEA asks for 45% in PCM, and 40% for BC, MBC and SC/ST. Your rank comes from Maths + Physics/2 + Chemistry/2, so a Maths mark counts double.

Third-party

Private universities at 50 to 60%

MET asks for 50%, SRM between 50% and 60% depending on the campus, and VIT 60%. Entrance scores, fees and seat counts decide more than the cut-off.

Third-party

Top 20 percentile of your board

JoSAA accepts being in the top 20 percentile of your board instead of 75%. The board cut-off is published after results, so it cannot be planned on in advance.

Third-party

DASA and CIWG for NRI students

Check which percentage and which subjects your year's brochure counts. The sources we found disagree between 60% and 75%.

Our judgement

Ask before you drop a year

JEE Main allows 3 consecutive years after Class 12, but a repeat year is a large decision. Talk through marks, rank targets and costs with a counsellor first.

Questions · Students and parents

Quick answers.

Reports on the 2026 cycle describe the 75% rule as an admission rule and not an exam rule. It applies when a seat is formally allotted through JoSAA. You can sit the exams and then need the percentage, or a top-20-percentile place in your board, at that stage.

Subject totals on a CBSE mark sheet include practical or internal marks, so this page counts them. Some bodies may count a different set of subjects. Read the notice for your route.

Plan for both. JoSAA is described as a five-subject aggregate. Most private and state routes in the table use PCM. A strong English or fifth-subject score can lift the five-subject figure above PCM, as in the example numbers.

News reports say CBSE cancelled Class 12 exams in seven West Asian countries in March 2026 (UAE, Bahrain, Iran, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and Saudi Arabia) and gave results under a special assessment scheme. It then held optional exams from 23 to 28 October 2026 for students who wanted to improve, and said the new result is final and replaces the earlier one. The reports we read do not say how JoSAA, DASA or any university treats the new marks. Ask each body in writing before you rely on them.
