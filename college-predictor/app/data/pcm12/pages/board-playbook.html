Step 1 · Find your level

The same syllabus asks for a different plan at each level.

Pick the band that matches your last two theory tests in each subject. Students often sit in different bands for different subjects, so read two panels if you need to.

Target by the board exam

Pass every paper with room to spare, then cross 60% in PCM.

Our judgement

What the sheets usually show

  • Questions left blank, mostly long answers and case studies.
  • Final answer written with no formula and no unit.
  • Diagrams skipped or unlabelled.

Do first

  • Work the solved examples in NCERT for every Core chapter, one chapter at a time.
  • Keep a one-page formula and unit sheet per chapter and read it for 10 minutes daily.
  • Attempt every Section A question and write at least the formula and substitution in Sections B and C.
  • Protect the 80 marks from practicals and internals. They are the most reachable marks you have.

Leave for later

  • Alternating Current in Physics, and stretch problems in every chapter marked Build.

Target by the board exam

Reach 65 to 75% in PCM by removing calculation and completeness slips.

Our judgement

What the sheets usually show

  • Correct method, but arithmetic and sign errors cost a mark in most numericals.
  • Derivations stop one step early.
  • Too long spent on the last 5 mark question, then a rushed Section C.

Do first

  • Start an error log. For every mistake in a test, write the cause: concept, calculation, reading, or time.
  • Practise section by section against the clock before attempting full papers.
  • Write out the derivation list for each subject and rehearse each one on paper twice.
  • Learn name reactions and conversions in Chemistry as a table, not as paragraphs.

Leave for later

  • Stretch problems beyond the NCERT exercise level until Section A to C are steady.

Target by the board exam

Move into the 80 to 90 band by protecting step marks and case-study marks.

Our judgement

What the sheets usually show

  • Right answers with steps compressed, so the examiner cannot give method marks.
  • Case-based questions answered from memory, not from the passage.
  • Assertion-reason questions lost to careless reading.

Do first

  • Attempt previous year and sample papers under exam conditions, then mark yourself against the marking scheme.
  • Rewrite two low-scoring answers a week in full marking-scheme style.
  • Do a timed Section A drill twice a week, with a 5-minute review for assertion-reason items.
  • Close the weakest chapter in each subject before adding anything new.

Target by the board exam

Make the paper error-free: 90 and above comes from not losing the marks you already know.

Our judgement

What the sheets usually show

  • Haste in easy questions, such as a missed unit or a dropped sign.
  • Neat solutions but a gap between the question wording and what was answered.
  • No time left for a final review.

Do first

  • Simulate the full paper twice a week and aim to finish 20 minutes early for review.
  • Read NCERT Chemistry line by line. Many marks in Chemistry sit in one NCERT sentence.
  • Practise competency-style questions and exemplar problems after every chapter.
  • Keep a short list of your personal slips and tick it off before handing in each practice paper.

Paper design follows the pattern in recent CBSE sample papers. Internal choice is given in parts of Sections B to E. Check the instructions on your own paper and the 2027 sample paper when CBSE releases it. Minutes are our suggested budget.

Rule

Formula, substitute, answer

Write the formula, put in the numbers with units, then the answer with its unit. Examiners can give a mark for each line.

Rule

Bank the sure marks first

Take Section A fast and mark the questions you will return to. Then go in section order and keep to the clock.

Rule

Draw in pencil, label in full

Ray diagrams, circuits, Gaussian surfaces, graphs. Use a ruler, add arrows and name every point.

Rule

One step per line

Maths and Chemistry answers earn marks line by line. Name the identity or reaction you are using.

Rule

Read the case study twice

Underline the data, then answer from the passage. Case-based items test reading as much as recall.

Rule

Write for a screen

Your pages are scanned. Use a dark blue or black pen, leave a margin and strike out rough work neatly.

Rule

Choose your choice once

When a question has an internal choice, attempt the option you want marked. If you start the other, strike it out clearly.

Rule

Keep 20 minutes to check

Check units, signs, constants such as +C, question numbers and any unanswered part.

Step 5 · How sheets are checked

Your answer book is scanned, then marked on a screen.

From the 2026 exams, CBSE Class 12 answer books are evaluated with on-screen marking. Here is how the process works, as described by CBSE in its February 2026 webcast and reported by Careers360.

  1. CollectAnswer books are collected at designated centres after the exam.
  2. ScanEach page is scanned at high resolution, including diagrams, graphs and rough work.
  3. MaskScripts are encrypted and uploaded with a unique digital identity. Candidate details stay hidden.
  4. AssignThe system sends scripts to subject evaluators at random.
  5. MarkThe examiner marks section by section on screen, with the marking scheme displayed and annotation tools.
  6. TotalMarks are totalled automatically, which removes addition errors.
  7. MonitorOfficials track progress and marking patterns across regions.

What the examiner is looking for

  • Value points from the marking scheme: the formula, the key step, the result and the unit.
  • A correct alternative method earns the same marks. This is a standard instruction in CBSE marking schemes.
  • Labelled diagrams where the question asks for one.
  • Keywords in theory answers, such as the reason after "because".

What quietly costs marks

  • A bare final answer. The examiner has nothing to give method marks against.
  • A missing or wrong unit, and a dropped sign.
  • Faint ink or crowded lines that scan badly.
  • Rough work mixed into the answer, which the scan will show.

Questions · Students and parents

Quick answers.

Physics and Chemistry practicals are 30 marks each and Maths internal assessment is 20, so 80 of 300 marks (about 27%). They are the most controllable marks, so full preparation for practicals, records and viva pays off in every cut-off that uses PCM marks.

CBSE board papers do not carry negative marking, so attempt every question. For MCQs, eliminate options and guess. For long answers, write what you know, because step marks are possible even when the final answer is wrong.

Every page of the answer book is scanned, so assume yes. Keep rough work in one clearly marked area, strike it through, and never mix it with the final answer.

Apply for the scanned copy first, check it against the marking scheme, then apply for verification or re-evaluation only for specific questions. The stages, fees and dates reported for 2026 are in the table above. Confirm the current notice before paying.

News reports say CBSE cancelled Class 12 exams in seven West Asian countries in March 2026 and gave results under a special assessment scheme that used exams already held and internal assessments such as pre-boards. It then held optional exams from 23 to 28 October 2026 for students who wanted to improve, and said the new result replaces the earlier one. Nobody can know what 2027 holds, so treat pre-boards and internals as real marks and keep your preparation on schedule.
