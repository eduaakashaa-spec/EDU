    How this is calculated

    Section marks = (4 × correct − wrong) ÷ (4 × questions) × section marks, never below zero. The three sections are added and multiplied by the difficulty factor to estimate a Paper 2B score out of 400. Gap = target − estimate. Marks per week = gap ÷ whole weeks left. Gap classes: small up to 50, medium 51 to 120, large over 120. The share of the gap given to each section follows its unused marks. These are planning rules of thumb, not a rank or cut-off prediction.
