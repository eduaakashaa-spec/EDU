    ChanceCollegeCourseSeat usedCut-offPrev. yearRegion
    How the chances are worked out. Your percentile and each closing percentile are turned into state merit ranks with that year's official percentile-to-rank data. Safe: your rank is at least 20% better than the closing rank. Likely: within the closing rank. Reach: up to 30% beyond it; such seats often open up in Rounds II to IV. The predictor uses the open seat plus your category, ladies seats (for women), your home-university seats at non-autonomous colleges, EWS and TFWS where they apply. It leaves out minority, PwD, Defence and Orphan seats and the All India and institute quotas. Cut-offs change every year and Round I is not the last round: treat this as a guide, not a promise of a seat.
