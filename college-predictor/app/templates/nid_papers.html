How to read this

Open tasks are not marked by a computer, but they are where most of the marks sit in the official B.Des sample (75 of 100). They reward the ability to tell a story in frames, to look at a problem from a user’s side and to produce several different ideas fast. Practise them on paper with a timer, then ask someone to mark them against a rubric: the Studio section of the mock centre gives {{ 30 }} tasks and rubrics for exactly that.
