Why this matters
  • Not every topic is worth the same. In 2026 the chapters were grouped into Tiers by how many questions they brought. Tier 1 chapters came up in almost every shift, Tier 3 only now and then, and some chapters had no question at all.
  • A gap costs marks according to its weight. A rating of 1 in a Tier 1 chapter loses more marks every paper than the same rating in a Tier 3 chapter. Study time that follows the gap map goes where the marks are.
  • Negative marking makes weak topics expensive. A topic you cannot solve is a topic you skip or guess. Closing a Tier 1 or Tier 2 gap turns skipped questions into safe marks.
  • It is an estimate. "Marks within reach" is questions per paper x 4 x the gap that remains to the level JEE Main needs. It guides where to study; it does not predict a score.
