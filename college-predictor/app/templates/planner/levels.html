How the planner works

Read this once. Every plan is built from the same simple ideas, and every task explains itself.

  1. Choose your exam and level. Not sure? Take the 1-minute check below.
  2. Get a day-by-day plan. One subject a day in rotation, one chapter at a time, a rest day on Sunday and a mock test on Saturday.
  3. Tick tasks when they are finished. The dashboard shows your streak, progress and hours.
  4. Practise in EduPulse. Practice tasks open EduPulse; your answers there count toward your week, and weak chapters turn into revision tasks.
  5. Review every week. Reports show what to change. Your mentor sees the same numbers and can adjust the plan.

Using EduPulse questions with the planner

  • For JEE, practice tasks open EduPulse Reinforcement (if your mentor has added you) or the JEE mock centre.
  • For UCEED, practice tasks open EduPulse-UCEED chapter practice and mocks open the UCEED mock centre.
  • Questions you answer in EduPulse are counted automatically on your dashboard and in reports. For NEET there is no EduPulse bank yet, so tick tasks yourself.
  • When EduPulse marks a chapter as weak, press Add revision tasks on the dashboard.
