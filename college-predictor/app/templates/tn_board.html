    About the data. "TN Matriculation Board" schools follow the common Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) HSC syllabus, so this page maps the State Board Class 11 and 12 textbooks. JEE Main question counts and Tiers come from our chapter tagging of all 19 JEE Main 2026 shifts (the same data as the EduPulse JEE analysis). NEET counts are indicative averages per paper from the 2022 to 2026 papers under the NMC syllabus, rounded. Coverage means the topic is in the TN textbook, not that the TN book reaches JEE or NEET difficulty: the notes on each chapter say what to add. See also Class 12 PCM Analytics and Class 12 PCB Analytics for marks-based eligibility. Always check the current syllabus in the NTA information bulletins and the +2 timetable at dge.tn.gov.in.
