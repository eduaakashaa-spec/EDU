CAT and studio practice

These tasks restate what the NIFT B.Des and M.Des Creative Ability Tests asked in earlier years: design with given shapes, show a transformation, draw a scene from memory, express a feeling in colour, or write about an experience. A computer cannot mark a drawing, so you do it on paper, mark yourself against a four-line rubric, and show your mentor.
