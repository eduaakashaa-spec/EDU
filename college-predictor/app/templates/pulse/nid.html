The mocks follow the layout of NID’s own sample papers: B.Des Prelims has 15 questions of 1 mark and 5 of 2 marks in the objective part (25 marks), and M.Des CDAT Paper 1 has 16 of 1 mark and 2 of 2 marks (20 marks). NID prints no duration or negative marking on these samples, so we give a practice time of about 1.2 minutes a mark and deduct nothing. Confirm both, the stages and the weight of Prelims in the current NID admission guide and handbook. The questions are original EduAakashaA practice questions written for these topics; they are not NID’s questions. Scores are practice guidance, not a prediction.
