BITS Pilani does not publish its question papers. These are the memory-based papers circulated after each sitting, shown as pictures exactly as printed, with the answer key published alongside them: use them as practice, not as the official paper. A few questions whose picture or key could not be trusted are left out of the tests; each paper’s count says how many remain. Scores are practice guidance, not a prediction of your BITSAT score. The real test is computer-based, so try a full mock on a laptop with the timer on.
