Where the numbers come from

  • Source: the National Medical Commission seat matrix for MBBS, academic year 2025–26, as on 29 October 2025 (819 colleges, including AIIMS, other institutes of national importance and JIPMER), and the earlier matrix as on 10 October 2025 (813 colleges), both supplied to EduAakashaA as PDFs.
  • Every row was read from the PDF text. The column totals equal the totals printed on the last page of each notice: 117,750 seats in 2024–25, and 128,875 (29 October) or 126,600 (10 October) in 2025–26.
  • Seats change for several reasons: new colleges, renewal of existing seats, increases and cuts. The notice itself says that 456 seats were reduced during renewal. The matrix shows the result for each college, not the reason.
  • Management is as printed (government, trust, society, private and so on). “Government” on this page also counts government-society colleges such as GMERS. A few colleges changed their printed name between the two notices, so they may appear as new in the comparison.
  • These files hold no counselling data: no NEET scores or ranks, no closing ranks, no round-wise allotments, no fees and no quota split. For those, read the counselling authority’s notices (MCC for the all-India quota, your state for the state quota).
  • A seat in the matrix is not a seat you will be offered: eligibility, domicile, category and counselling choices decide that.
