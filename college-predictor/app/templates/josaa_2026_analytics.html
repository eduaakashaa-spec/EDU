Where the numbers come from

  • Ranks: JoSAA opening and closing ranks, Rounds 1 to 5, for 138 institutes (IIT, IISc, NIT+, IIIT and GFTI). The file supplied is named Round 1 to 6 but holds Rounds 1 to 5 only; Round 6 was not in it.
  • Seats: the NIT+ seat matrix (31 NITs and IIEST Shibpur): 25,162 seats. Every NIT+ rank cell matched a seat cell.
  • The files give the opening and closing rank of each cell, not how many candidates were allotted, so fill rates cannot be worked out.
  • Rise (shift) is the Round 5 closing rank minus the Round 1 closing rank, on numeric ranks only. A rise means more candidates got in (relaxed); a fall means tightened.
  • 40 rows show an opening rank above the closing rank: all are cells that mix a preparatory ("P") closing rank with a regular opening rank, not errors.
  • The latest closing rank is the last-round view; real allotment also depends on withdrawals, spot rounds and your choice order. Treat every figure as guidance and confirm at josaa.nic.in.
