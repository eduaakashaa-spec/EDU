IPM basics

What is the five-year IPM?

The Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) is a five-year course offered by some Indian Institutes of Management straight after Class 12. Students study foundation and management subjects together and, depending on the IIM, receive an integrated degree with exit options along the way. Entry is through an aptitude test followed by further rounds.

Official on an official site   Reported — verify news / coaching sources or earlier cycles; confirm in the notice   Planning guide EduAakashaA suggestion, not an official rule

🏫

Who offers IPM?

IIM Indore (IPM since 2011) and IIM Rohtak run their own admissions tests; other IIMs, including IIM Bodh Gaya and IIM Jammu, admit through JIPMAT conducted by NTA. The exact list of IIMs and which scores each accepts can change — check each IIM’s notice.

Reported — verify
📝

Three main exams

IPMAT Indore, IPMAT Rohtak and JIPMAT. Each has its own registration, fee, pattern, eligibility and later rounds, so apply to each separately.

🧠

What is tested?

Quantitative ability and verbal ability are the common core; some exams add logical reasoning / data interpretation. It is aptitude-based, built on school-level maths and English.

Reported — verify

Five different steps — don’t mix them up

  1. Register & take the test Apply by the deadline and write each aptitude test you choose.
  2. Get a score and a shortlist Each IIM publishes results and shortlists candidates for the next round.
  3. Written ability test & personal interview Shortlisted candidates take the WAT / PI at the IIM (format set by each IIM).
  4. Final merit list The IIM combines test score, interview and, depending on the IIM, school marks and other factors.
  5. Accept the offer Only a seat offer — accepted and paid for within the deadline — is an admission.

Weightages differ by IIM and by year; read the admission procedure of each IIM you target.

Instructions for students

From Class 11 to an IIM seat: what to do, in order

Follow these steps for every exam you choose. Dates are intentionally not listed where no official notice exists — add each deadline to your calendar the day it is announced.

1 · Before you apply

  • Read the latest official notice or information bulletin of each exam you target.
  • Check eligibility: age on the stated cut-off date, Class 10 and 12 marks, and whether students appearing in Class 12 can apply.
  • Confirm your category (General, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD) and what certificate each IIM needs.
  • Create a dedicated email id and phone number you will use for every IIM and NTA login.
  • Keep scanned copies ready (see the documents checklist below).

2 · Registration & fee

  • Register only on the official IIM or NTA site — never through an agent or an unknown link.
  • Fill name, date of birth and marks exactly as in your certificates.
  • Upload photograph and signature in the size and format specified in the bulletin.
  • Pay the fee online and download the confirmation page.
  • Submit well before the last date; servers slow down on closing day.
  • Fees and any late fee differ each year — read the notice.

3 · Admit card & exam day

  • Download the admit card when released and check name, photo, centre and shift.
  • Carry the admit card and the photo ID named in the notice.
  • Reach the centre by the reporting time — IIM Indore’s 2026 notice said no candidate was allowed to report after 1:30 pm for a 2 pm shift.
  • Follow the rules on permitted items; do not carry electronic devices.
  • Manage time by section; skip hard questions and watch negative marking.

4 · After the test

  • Check the provisional answer key and the objection window, if offered.
  • Wait for the official result and shortlist; do not rely on social-media rank lists.
  • Complete any separate IIM application or document-upload step by its deadline.
  • Prepare for the written ability test (essay-style) and the personal interview.
  • Keep checking your registered email and the IIM portal daily during the process.

Selection rounds in plain words

  1. Aptitude test Quantitative and verbal ability (plus reasoning in some exams) decides who is shortlisted.
  2. Written ability test (WAT) A short essay on a topic, testing clarity, structure and argument. Practise with a timer.
  3. Personal interview (PI) Questions on your school, interests, current affairs and why management. Be honest and prepared.
  4. Final merit Combines the elements in the IIM’s own formula — read it in the admission procedure.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming one registration covers every exam.
  • Using unofficial websites or paying an agent to “guarantee” a seat — no one can.
  • Missing the last date because the portal was busy.
  • Practising only in one pattern and being surprised by another.
  • Ignoring Class 12 board preparation — school marks may count in eligibility or final merit.

FAQs

Questions students and parents ask

What is IPM?

The five-year Integrated Programme in Management offered by some IIMs after Class 12. You study foundation and management subjects together; the degree and exit options depend on the IIM.

Which exams lead to IPM?

IPMAT Indore (IIM Indore), IPMAT Rohtak (IIM Rohtak) and JIPMAT (NTA, for the IIMs that use it). Each is applied for separately.

When is IPMAT 2027?

No official 2027 date was available on 18 October 2026. Last year IIM Indore held IPMAT on 4 May 2026. Treat any 2027 date you see as an assumption until the IIM announces it.

Who is eligible?

Reported rules include a minimum percentage in Class 10 and 12 and an age ceiling, with students appearing in Class 12 allowed in some exams. Rules differ for each exam and for reserved categories — the official notice is final.

Can Class 11 students start now?

Yes. Class 11 students would normally apply in the next cycle. Build maths and reading foundations now; use the “next cycle” target in the roadmap, whose date is only a planning assumption.

Can I apply to IPMAT Indore, Rohtak and JIPMAT together?

Many students do, as the skills overlap. Each has its own registration, fee, format and eligibility, so confirm each and keep every deadline on your calendar.

Is maths very hard?

The maths is school-level but tested under time pressure and with problem-solving depth. Regular practice from Class 11 usually matters more than coaching volume.

Is there negative marking?

It has been reported for multiple-choice questions in these exams, and the rules differ. Read the current bulletin and practise with the same marking scheme.

What is the WAT and interview?

Shortlisted candidates write a short essay and then face a personal interview. They test communication, clarity and awareness. The IIM’s admission procedure explains how each round is weighted.

Does a good score guarantee a seat?

No. Seats go to candidates who are shortlisted, perform in later rounds and accept the offer within the deadline.

Do board marks matter?

They can matter for eligibility and, at some IIMs, for the final merit. Do not neglect Class 12.

How can EduAakashaA help me plan?

By assessing your starting point, setting weekly targets, reviewing practice and mock tests, and helping your family understand eligibility, rounds and deadlines. Scope is confirmed with our team — outcomes are never guaranteed.
