ELIGIBILITY

Who can appear

Conditions in recent bulletins. Re-check the 2027 bulletin, especially anything marked “confirm”.

{{ ea_icon('user') }}
Age

At least 17 years old on 31 December of the year of admission. Confirm in the bulletin whether any upper age limit applies; we did not find one stated in the sources we checked.

{{ ea_icon('file') }}
Qualification

Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology and English. Students appearing in 2027 are eligible to apply, subject to the result.

{{ ea_icon('chart') }}
Minimum marks

In recent bulletins: 50% in the PCB subjects for the General category, 45% for PwD and 40% for SC, ST and OBC candidates. Confirm the exact wording in the 2027 bulletin.

{{ ea_icon('award') }}
Attempts

No attempt limit was stated in the sources we checked. Confirm in the bulletin before relying on it.

SYLLABUS

What the paper covers

An indicative map by subject. The official syllabus is published by NMC and NTA with the bulletin; always study from that list.

{{ ea_icon('chart') }}
Physics (45 questions)

Mechanics and properties of matter, heat and thermodynamics, waves and oscillations, electrostatics and current electricity, magnetism and EMI, optics, modern physics, semiconductors and units and measurement. Numerical, formula-based questions reward speed.

{{ ea_icon('file') }}
Chemistry (45 questions)

Physical (mole concept, equilibrium, thermodynamics, electrochemistry, kinetics), inorganic (periodic table, bonding, coordination compounds, p-, d- and f-block) and organic (named reactions, mechanisms, biomolecules). Inorganic is largely NCERT-based.

{{ ea_icon('star') }}
Biology (90 questions)

Botany and Zoology together carry half of the marks: diversity of life, cell and molecular biology, genetics and evolution, plant and human physiology, reproduction, ecology and biotechnology. The paper follows NCERT closely.

{{ ea_icon('award') }}
WHERE MARKS COME FROM Biology is 360 of the 720 marks. A student who knows NCERT Biology line by line starts with a large, dependable block of marks, and many students build their cushion there.
PREPARATION PLAN

October 2026 to the exam, month by month

Built for an exam in early May 2027. Adjust the last two phases if NTA announces a different date.

Oct – Nov 2026Foundation
  • Read NCERT Biology cover to cover, with diagrams.
  • Rebuild Class 11 Physics and Chemistry basics.
  • Fix a daily routine: 6–8 focused hours plus school.
Dec 2026 – Jan 2027First full pass
  • Finish the whole syllabus once, chapter by chapter.
  • Solve topic-wise questions after every chapter.
  • Start an error log: topic, mistake, fix.
Feb 2027Apply and consolidate
  • Watch neet.nta.nic.in; keep documents ready for the form.
  • Take a mock every week and review it fully.
  • Second pass on Biology.
Mar 2027Revision and speed
  • Second pass on Chemistry and Physics.
  • Solve previous-year papers under timed conditions.
  • Rate your confidence on each answer to spot lucky guesses.
Apr 2027Mock-heavy
  • Two to three full mocks a week at the exam time of day.
  • Work the error log; revise only weak chapters.
  • Practise the OMR or on-screen routine you expect.
Last 2 weeksLight and steady
  • Short revision of formulas, reactions and diagrams.
  • Sleep on schedule; no new topics.
  • Check the admit card, centre and travel the day before.
AFTER THE RESULT

How counselling works

01
Result and rank
NTA publishes the result with the all-India rank. Category ranks and state ranks follow where applicable.
02
All India Quota (15%) through MCC
The Medical Counselling Committee (mcc.nic.in) runs counselling for the 15% all-India quota of government colleges, plus central and deemed universities and other institutions, in several rounds.
03
State quota (85%) through state authorities
Each state runs its own counselling for the state quota and private colleges, with its own domicile rules, fees and schedule. Tamil Nadu students follow the Tamil Nadu medical selection process.
04
Choice filling, allotment and reporting
Fill college and course choices in the order you prefer, accept the seat, pay the fee and report with original documents before the deadline.

BE READY

Documents and habits to prepare now

{{ ea_icon('file') }}
Documents

Class 10 certificate (date of birth), Class 12 marksheet or school bonafide, recent photograph, signature, photo ID, and category or PwD certificate if applicable. The bulletin lists the exact formats and sizes.

{{ ea_icon('link') }}
Official sites only

Apply only on neet.nta.nic.in and follow counselling on mcc.nic.in and your state site. NTA does not ask for money through calls or messages.

{{ ea_icon('chart') }}
Error log

Write every wrong answer with the reason: concept gap, careless slip or guess. Review it weekly; this is the quickest way to add marks.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

When is NEET UG 2027?

NTA has not announced it. Recent exams were held on the first Sunday of May, so early May 2027 is the planning assumption. The official date will be in the information bulletin on neet.nta.nic.in.

When will registration open?

Not announced. In recent cycles the notice and form came a few months before the exam, so February to March 2027 is a reasonable expectation. Keep your documents ready.

Will NEET 2027 be computer-based?

The 2026 exam was pen-and-paper. A change to computer-based testing has been reported, but it is not confirmed until NTA states it in the bulletin. Practise with both OMR-style and on-screen mocks.

Is there negative marking?

Yes, in the latest pattern: +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one. Unattempted questions score zero. Confirm in the 2027 bulletin.

Which books should I use?

NCERT first, especially for Biology and Inorganic Chemistry, then one problem book per subject and previous-year papers. Avoid collecting many books; finish a few.

Can I appear if I am in Class 12 now?

Yes. Students appearing in the Class 12 board exam in 2027 have been eligible to apply, subject to meeting the minimum marks after the board result. Check the bulletin.

How does a mentor help?

A mentor builds a weekly plan around your school schedule, reviews your mocks and error log, and helps with the counselling choices later: state quota, all-India quota and the colleges that fit your rank.
