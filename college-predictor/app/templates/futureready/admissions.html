Always confirm on the official website. Eligibility, dates, fees and seat rules change every year. Anything marked “Requires verification” has not been checked against the official notice for a specific year, so treat it as general awareness only.

The big picture

  • Entrance examination: a test that produces a score or rank (for example JEE Main).
  • Counselling process: where seats are allotted to candidates using a score, rank or merit list (for example JoSAA).
  • Merit-based admission process: admission using published merit rules, such as Class 12 marks, without a separate entrance exam (for example TNEA in Tamil Nadu).
  • Institution exam: a university's own entrance test.

One exam does not decide a career: there are many routes, and several branches lead to similar careers.
