What we collect

  • Name and e-mail (from the account), grade, board, school (optional).
  • Answers to assessments, goals, actions and mentor notes.
  • We do not collect phone numbers, addresses, photographs or location for students.

Who sees what

  • Student: their own results, plan and shared notes.
  • Parent / guardian: results, reports and shared notes for linked children; never private mentor notes; never a student's open-text answers.
  • Assigned mentor: only their own students. Private mentor notes are never shown to the student or parent.
  • Administrator: all records, with an audit log of sensitive actions.

Consent

Assessments stay locked until a parent or guardian records consent. Consent can be withdrawn by writing to info@eduaakashaa.com; on request we delete the child's FutureReady records.

Fairness and responsible AI

  • Results are labelled as snapshots; self-report answers are kept apart from tested results.
  • Recommendations are rule-based, show the evidence behind them and are reviewed by a mentor before families see them.
  • We never rank children, predict a branch or admission, or give cut-offs.
  • If a student seems distressed, mentors are asked to involve the parent and, where appropriate, a qualified school counsellor. FutureReady is not a mental-health service.
