What FutureReady is

FutureReady helps students in Grades 8 and 9 understand their strengths, build strong foundations and explore engineering through projects, with a mentor and parents alongside.

  • It shows where you are today; it does not label you.
  • It never ranks students or predicts a college or branch.
  • You can stop any time, and your mentor explains every result.

Please read the privacy and child-safety page.

Parent or guardian consent

FutureReady collects a child's learning information. A parent or guardian must agree before assessments open.

  • We store answers, goals, actions and mentor notes only for mentoring this student.
  • Private mentor notes are never shown to the student or parent; parents do not see a student's open-text answers.
  • Records can be deleted on request (info@eduaakashaa.com).
  • Results are snapshots, not predictions.
