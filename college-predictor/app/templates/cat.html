What changed this year

New conducting IIM, a revised fee (₹2,700 General / ₹1,350 SC/ST/PwBD, up from ₹2,600 / ₹1,300 in 2025), about 170 test cities with five choices, NC-OBC certificates issued on or after 1 April 2026, and a CAT score valid until 31 December 2027. The exam pattern, eligibility and marking scheme are unchanged. If you could not register, the window has closed — plan for CAT 2027 or alternative exams (XAT, SNAP, CMAT, NMAT, GMAT).

PROCESS

CAT Exam Process Explained

1
Registration (closed)
Opened 3 Aug 2026 at iimcat.ac.in and closed 22 Sep 2026, 5 PM, after a one-week extension.
2
Application
Select up to 5 test cities. Fee: ₹2,700 (General / EWS / NC-OBC) · ₹1,350 (SC / ST / PwBD).
3
Admit Card
Downloadable from 4 Nov 2026 until the exam; carry a printed copy with a photo ID.
4
CBT Exam
Sun 29 Nov 2026 · 120 minutes · 3 sections · 68 questions · 204 marks.
5
Results
Expected between December 2026 and the first week of January 2027. Sectional + overall percentile.
6
WAT / PI / GD
IIMs shortlist for written analysis, personal interview, group discussion.
7
Selection
Composite = CAT + academics + work experience + diversity.
8
Admission
Accept offer and begin your MBA journey.

MCQs and TITA (type-in-the-answer) questions · +3 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong MCQ, no negative marking for TITA · each section is timed separately · PwBD candidates get extra time. Eligibility: a bachelor’s degree with 50% (45% for SC/ST/PwBD); final-year students may apply. Section-wise question counts are as widely reported for 2025–26; confirm in the information bulletin.

NRI / OVERSEAS

International Applicant Guide

1
Eligibility
50% in graduation. Foreign degrees need AIU equivalence.
2
CAT or GMAT
CAT conducted only in India. GMAT (650–720+) worldwide.
3
Apply
Submit with passport, visa, NRI certificate.
4
Interview
Virtual WAT/PI. Overseas centres in Dubai / Singapore.
5
Enroll
NRI fees higher. Wire transfer accepted. 5–15% seats reserved.
  • IIMs accepting GMAT: IIM-A PGPX, IIM-B EPGP, IIM-C PGPEX, ISB Hyderabad PGP
  • Typical NRI fees: ₹25–35 LPA (higher than domestic)
  • Student visa required before enrolment
