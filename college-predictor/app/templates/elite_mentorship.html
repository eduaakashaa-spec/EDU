Two different jobs

Treat mentorship as seriously as coaching.

Coaching and mentoring do not compete. Most students need both. The gap opens when coaching is the only support and nobody owns the decisions.

Where it starts

Onboarding assessment for student and parents: marks and mock trend, an eligibility audit (residency, passport, CIWG status, the 75% rule), study habits, interests and family expectations.

Branch Fit Assessment

30 questions, about 20 minutes. It scores fit across seven branch families, then matches the top fits against real closing ranks and the outlook for each branch.

Industry 4.0 and AI briefing

How each branch is changing, and the skills to build in year 1. The question shifts from “which branch is safe?” to “which branch fits, taught the modern way?”

Questions students and parents ask

Straight answers.

Does mentorship replace my coaching?

No. Coaching builds your marks and percentile; mentorship decides what they are for. Most students need both, and the mentor plans around your coaching timetable.

Is Grade 11 too early to think about admissions?

Eligibility, the document trail and the exam calendar all begin in Grade 11. It is also the one year with no deadline sitting on top of the decisions, so they can be made calmly.

How do the sessions happen?

Online, one to one. In the Grade 11 track the mentor meets the student and the parents together on Microsoft Teams four times in the year, and parents are briefed in writing at every gate. The Grade 12 track has ten or more sessions.

Is this only for NRI students?

The programme was built around families abroad who use the DASA and CIWG routes, and it covers JoSAA, state and private routes in parallel. Students in India can ask about the plans listed on the Members Registration page.

Will the mentor fill my forms or guarantee a seat?

Forms and choices are prepared and verified with you; you submit them. EduAakashaA cannot and does not guarantee a rank or a seat: seats are allotted by the authorities on rank and eligibility.

What if a worry falls outside mentorship?

We say so plainly and point you to the right help.
