Law entrance overview

Understand the law-entrance landscape first

After Class 12, most students enter law through a five-year integrated programme (B.A. LL.B. and similar) at a law university. Each university group runs its own entrance test — so the key is knowing which tests you can take, when, and what each one leads to.

Official on an official site   Reported — verify news reports / earlier cycles; confirm in the exam brochure   Planning guide EduAakashaA suggestion, not an official rule

⚖️

What is CLAT?

The Common Law Admission Test, run by the Consortium of National Law Universities, is the main route into the member NLUs for the five-year programme (and, separately, LL.M.).

26 member NLUs listed officially
🏛️

Why more than one test?

NLU Delhi runs its own AILET; Symbiosis runs SLAT; some states and universities have their own tests. Each has separate dates, fees, eligibility and counselling.

🧠

What do they test?

Reading comprehension, current affairs and general knowledge, legal reasoning, logical reasoning and — in most — quantitative techniques. It is skill-based, not school-syllabus recall.

Reported — verify

Four different steps — don’t mix them up

  1. Take the test Register by the deadline and write each exam you choose.
  2. Get a score and rank Results and merit lists are published by each exam body.
  3. Counselling / application CLAT counselling ranks your preferences across NLUs; other universities run their own applications and rounds.
  4. Receive and accept an offer Only a seat offer — accepted and paid for on time — is an admission.

A rank is not a seat. CLAT counselling dates for this cycle have not been announced yet Awaited.

FAQs

Questions students and parents ask

What is CLAT?

The Common Law Admission Test run by the Consortium of National Law Universities. It is the main entrance route to the five-year integrated law programmes at member NLUs.

Who is eligible for CLAT 2027?

Reported rules: Class 12 (or equivalent) with 45% (40% for SC/ST), and students appearing in 2027 may apply with proof of passing at admission; no upper age limit was reported. The official 2027 notice is the final authority — please confirm it.

Can Class 11 students start preparing?

Yes. Class 11 students would normally take the exams in the next cycle. Reading widely, building vocabulary and following current affairs are useful from now. Use the “next cycle” target in the roadmap — its date is a planning assumption until it is announced.

Can I write CLAT, AILET and SLAT together?

Many students do, since the announced dates fall in December and the skills overlap. Each has its own registration, fee, format and eligibility, so confirm each one and keep the deadlines on your calendar.

Do I need to be good at maths?

Quantitative questions are a smaller part of most law tests and are usually school-level. Regular practice is enough for most students; check each exam’s current pattern.

How should I start if I do not read much?

Begin with 20–30 minutes a day of an editorial or long-form article, summarise it in your own words, and build up. Phase 3 of the roadmap is designed for this.

How much time should I spend each week?

There is no official number. As a planning guide many school-going students begin with 6–10 hours a week and increase closer to the exam. Use the planner and honestly review what you completed.

Are previous-year papers useful?

Yes — for the style of passages and questions, timing and your own error patterns. They are practice material, not a prediction of this year’s paper.

Which universities use CLAT?

The Consortium lists 26 member NLUs on its official site (see the table above); the notice for each year states participation, seats and rules.

Does a good score guarantee a seat?

No. Seats are allocated by rank, preferences, category and availability through the official counselling or application process.

Is a separate application or counselling needed?

Yes. For CLAT, registration for the test is separate from the later counselling where you rank NLU preferences; other universities run their own processes. Dates for the 2027 cycle will be announced after the exams.
