How the NIRF score is built

ScoreWeightMade up of (marks on the Architecture chart)
TLR: Teaching, learning and resources0.40Student strength (20), faculty-student ratio (30), faculty qualification and experience (20), financial resources and their use (30)
RPC: Research and professional practice0.20Publications (60), quality of publications (20), projects and professional practice (20)
GO: Graduation outcomes0.20Placement and higher studies (40), university examinations (30), median salary (30)
OI: Outreach and inclusivity0.10Students from other states and countries (30), women (30), economically and socially challenged students (20), facilities for physically challenged students (20)
PR: Perception0.10A survey of employers and academic peers (100)

Reading the 39 rating charts we hold (of 40): almost every institution earns full or near-full marks for accessibility, women, examinations and faculty-student ratio, and loses most marks on research income, publications, support for economically challenged students and perception. The scores that separate institutions most are financial resources, publications and perception.
