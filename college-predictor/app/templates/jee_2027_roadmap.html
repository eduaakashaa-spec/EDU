Route C · NRI, OCI, PIO, foreign nationals

NRI students can use JoSAA and DASA

Direct Admission of Students Abroad (DASA) fills supernumerary seats at NITs, IIITs, IIEST, SPAs and other GFTIs, on JEE Main rank. Since 2025, DASA runs together with the CSAB Special Rounds on csab.nic.in, after JoSAA ends. IITs are not part of DASA. Validated against the DASA 2026 brochure

Yes · JoSAA

NRIs can join JoSAA

NRI and OCI/PIO candidates can take part in JoSAA with their JEE Main (and JEE Advanced) rank, like any other candidate.

Yes · DASA after JoSAA

A JoSAA seat does not block DASA

Candidates who secured an NIT+ seat in JoSAA (SAF and PAF paid) can register for DASA as fresh candidates and keep that seat. IIT seat holders can also register.

Note · CSAB

One portal, one registration

DASA and CSAB-Special share a single process on csab.nic.in. In 2026 it had two allotment rounds (it had three in 2024 and 2025). If DASA allots a new seat, the earlier JoSAA seat is cancelled.

DASA-CIWG

Children of Indian Workers in Gulf

  1. Check CIWG eligibilityNRI student with one or both parents working in UAE, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar or Saudi Arabia.
  2. Prepare documentsPassport, NRI proof, parent’s Gulf employment proof, Class 10–12 transcripts and JEE Main scorecard.
  3. Register and fill choices Late JulChoose the DASA-CIWG tag on csab.nic.in, pay the DEF, upload documents and lock choices.
  4. Seat allotment AugOne-third of DASA seats are reserved for CIWG. CIWG candidates are also considered for Non-CIWG seats.
  5. Accept and report AugPay the fee, clear document verification and report to the allotted institute.
DASA · Non-CIWG

NRI, OCI, PIO and foreign nationals

  1. Check DASA eligibilityNRIs and OCI/PIO card holders from any country get the DASA (Non-SAARC) tag. Foreign nationals of SAARC countries get the DASA (SAARC) tag.
  2. Prepare documentsPassport or OCI/PIO card, NRI proof, Class 10–12 transcripts and JEE Main scorecard.
  3. Register and fill choices Late JulChoose the DASA tag on csab.nic.in, pay the DEF, upload documents and lock choices.
  4. Seat allotment AugSeats are allotted on JEE Main rank, category and choices. SAARC nationals get a 50% tuition fee waiver.
  5. Accept and report AugPay the fee (charged in USD), clear document verification and report to the allotted institute.

Alternate map · Below 75% in Class 12

Scored under 75%? You still have routes

IITs, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs admitted through JoSAA need 75% in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD) or a place in the top 20 percentile of your board. The rule applies at admission only. You can still sit JEE Main and JEE Advanced, and your rank is not affected by board marks.

Take JEE Main 2027 as plannedClass 12 aggregate (five subjects) below 75%, or below 65% for SC/ST/PwD.

Alternate map · NRI, studied in India

Parents abroad, school in India

DASA looks at where the student studied, not where the parents work. An Indian national must complete at least two years of education abroad, including Classes 11 and 12, within the last eight years, and pass Class 12 from abroad. This applies to CIWG too.

Indian passport

Not eligible for DASA or CIWG

The study-abroad rule is not met, even if a parent works in the Gulf. Apply as a regular Indian candidate.

OCI or PIO card

Check the DASA brochure

The 2026 brochure applies the same study-abroad rule to OCI and PIO candidates who choose Indian-national status (OCI/PIO (I)). Confirm your case with the 2027 brochure before planning on DASA.

Foreign passport

DASA is open

Foreign nationals studying in any country, including India, can apply. Follow Route C in this map.

Indian passport · your route: same process as any Indian student

Register for JEE Main from India

Use the Step 01 registration route. Pick exam cities near your school, not where your parents live.

JEE Advanced, if you qualify

Route A for IITs. No separate NRI process.

JoSAA, then CSAB-Special

Seats are allotted on your rank and category like any Indian candidate.

Home State is where you passed Class 12

NIT Home State quota follows the state of your Class 12 board exam, so studying in India gives you a Home State.

State counselling

You may be eligible in the state where you studied, for example TNEA in Tamil Nadu. Each state sets its own residence rules.

NRI and NRI-sponsored quotas

Many private and deemed universities, and some state systems, keep seats for NRI wards or NRI-sponsored candidates. Rules, fees and eligibility differ by institute.
