What the numbers say

    About the data. Closing marks for 2026 are worked out from the official TNEA 2026 Round 1, 2 and 3 general academic allotment lists (DOTE Chennai), read in full: {{ '{:,}'.format(data.n_seats) }} allotments, {{ data.n_col }} colleges, {{ data.n_rows }} college-branch rows. The closing mark of a course is the lowest cut-off allotted a seat in that community; the "final" 2026 mark is the lowest across the three rounds. College and branch names come from the official vacancy list after Round 1. The official cut-off site was not reachable in November 2026, so the 2025 column is the portal's own TNEA 2025 final cut-off sheet: it is shown for reference and could not be checked against an official list. TNEA 2027 dates are expected, following 2026. NIRF: India Rankings 2025, Engineering. Always confirm on tneaonline.org.

    How the chances are worked out. For each course the predictor takes the lower (easier) of the OC closing mark and your community's closing mark in the year or round you choose. Safe: your cut-off is at least 3 marks above it. Likely: at or up to 3 marks above. Reach: up to 3 marks below it; such seats sometimes open in a later round. It leaves out the Govt school 7.5%, special reservation and vocational seats. Closing marks change every year: use this as a guide, not a promise of a seat.
