What these tools can and cannot do

They organise your time and show where marks usually sit. They do not predict your result, and a plan only works if you follow it. Entrance-route percentages come from public sources: confirm each one in the official brochure or notice for your year. Chapter marks are indicative; only the unit-group totals are official. The sheets in the Answer Sheet Lab are illustrations written by EduAakashaA in CBSE style; they are not CBSE papers or real students' scripts. Prepared with AI assistance by EduAakashaA.
