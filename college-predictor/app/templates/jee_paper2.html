Part 3: One drawing, ten minutes (for 2A)

Take a plain A4 sheet and a pencil. Sketch a busy street market on a festival evening from memory. Show at least three people, one building and something in the foreground. Then ask yourself:

  • Did the sketch look balanced, with a near and a far part?
  • Did the people look the right size next to the building?
  • Did I enjoy it, or did I watch the clock?

If you enjoyed it and the sizes looked right, Paper 2A suits you. If it felt like a chore, look closely at Paper 2B. This is a self-check, not a score.
