What the numbers say

    About the data. NIRF publishes the Architecture and Planning ranking for the 40 top institutes. The 2026 edition had not been published when this page was built, so every number here is from the India Rankings 2025 Architecture and Planning list, the latest official one, and the page is labelled NIRF 2026 only to keep it alongside the engineering page. Overall score is the weighted total; the five parameter scores are each out of 100. Source: nirfindia.org. NIRF ranks institutes, not admission cut-offs: check the entrance exam (JEE Main Paper 2, NATA, JEE Advanced with AAT), fees and Council of Architecture approval for the college you pick.
