      Rank explorer · your numbers

      Where does your rank land?

      Enter your DASA CRL rank, pick your quota, and see every program sorted into Safe, Realistic or Stretch using 2026 closing ranks, with a check on whether it also worked in 2025. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

      How bands work. Safe: 2026 closing rank is at least 25% weaker than yours. Realistic: closing rank is between yours and 25% weaker. Stretch: closing rank is up to 20% better than yours (you'd need later-round movement). Out of reach: further than that. A program that worked in both years is a sturdier choice than one that worked in only one.
      What to do with this · for Gulf and NRI families

      Six habits for choice-filling

      Closing ranks describe the past. They are a guide to building a balanced list, not a promise about next round.

      01 · Build in bands

      Mix Dream, Realistic and Safe, and fill all of them.

      Because round 1 decided about 82% of programs in 2026, the choices you rank highest matter most. Put real preferences at the top, but keep enough Safe options below them that a missed seat doesn't leave you with nothing.

      02 · Use both years

      Prefer programs that worked twice.

      With over half of matched programs moving by more than 25% in a year, a seat that was within reach in both 2025 and 2026 is a sturdier bet than one that was within reach once.

      03 · Respect your quota

      CIWG and Non-CIWG are different markets.

      Don't carry intuition across. The same branch can be tightening for CIWG applicants while loosening for Non-CIWG.

      04 · Don't chase a one-year spike

      Treat extreme movers as noise until proven otherwise.

      A program that fell 70% or rose 400% probably has very few seats. Ask what happened before treating it as a trend.

      05 · Watch new programs

      104 programs have no history.

      New programs show a 2026 closing rank only. Use them as additions to a list you've already anchored with matched programs, and look at the institute's track record too.

      06 · Verify, then decide

      Confirm eligibility, fees and seat matrix on the official DASA portal.

      Branch choice, campus, fees and career goals matter as much as a rank. A counsellor can help you weigh them against these numbers, and we are happy to walk through your list with you.

      How we computed it
      • Change = 2026 final closing rank ÷ 2025 final closing rank − 1. Positive means weaker ranks got in (easier). Easier / Stable / Tougher uses a ±5% band.
      • Medians, not averages. Medians stop a few extreme programs from dominating a branch or institute.
      • Matching. Programs are matched on institute, program and quota. Renamed programs were matched by hand where possible; unmatched ones show as New in 2026 or 2025 only.
      • Round 1 check. Because the final round differs by year, we also compare Round 1 closing ranks. The direction agrees by quota and by branch.
      • Bands in the explorer are a simple rule of thumb on 2026 closing ranks, not a probability.
      Limits to keep in mind
      • Different round counts. 2025 had three rounds, 2026 had two. Later rounds can loosen closing ranks, so some 2026 figures may sit slightly tighter than a third round would have produced.
      • No seat counts. A program with 2 seats and one with 40 are weighted the same here.
      • Open seats only. Category, gender-specific and supernumerary seats are not included.
      • B.Arch / B.Plan use a separate rank list and must not be compared with engineering ranks.
      • Past ≠ future. Applicants, seats and choices change every year. Always confirm on the official DASA website.
