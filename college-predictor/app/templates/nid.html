What changed from last year

The undergraduate entry is now described as the 5.5 Year Professional Education Programme — Master of Design (Integrated Pathway) and B.Des., with a single application. The DAT Prelims result is on 16 March 2027, and DAT Mains uses a Studio Sensitivity Test plus an In-Person Sensitivity Test. The Prelims score is used only to shortlist — it carries no weightage into Mains.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Two-stage DAT: Prelims → Mains → Admission

1
Online application
One form on admissions.nid.edu (10 Sep – 30 Nov 2026) covers DAT Prelims and Mains. Upload photograph, signature and category / PwD / passport documents as applicable. B.Des / Integrated applicants cannot also apply for the independent 2.5-year M.Des — both tests are on the same day.
2
Pay the application fee
General, Gen-EWS, OBC-NCL: ₹3,000 · Female (except SC/ST/PwD): ₹2,000 · SC / ST: ₹1,500 · PwD and Third Gender: ₹500 · Overseas: US$125. Non-refundable and non-transferable; paid by debit / credit card or net banking; bank charges extra.
3
DAT Prelims — 20 Dec 2026
A paper-and-pencil/pen test, text and visual questions, in English only; the duration is printed on the admit card. Held in 17 cities: Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Chennai, Dehradun, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Jorhat, Kochi, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai, Nagpur, New Delhi and Patna.
4
Prelims result — 16 Mar 2027
Separate merit lists per category. For each category, 2.5× the available seats are shortlisted for Mains. Re-totalling window: 16–17 March 2027 (arithmetic check only, no re-evaluation).
5
DAT Mains
Studio Sensitivity Test + In-Person Sensitivity Test, details and admit cards to be announced. Final merit is based on Mains marks only; both parts must be attempted to be on the merit list.
6
Results, allotment & reporting
Final results and campus-wise admission follow the Mains — dates to be announced on the official website. Each NID uses the Mains score for its own campus.

OVERSEAS / NRI

International Applicants Guide

  • Overseas (supernumerary) category: for foreign citizens only — candidates holding citizenship of a country other than India. An additional 15% supernumerary seats are available: 15 seats (one per discipline) for the Ahmedabad / Gandhinagar / Bengaluru programme, and 1–2 per discipline at NID Madhya Pradesh and NID Haryana.
  • Fee and age: US$125 application fee (converted to rupees at the time of admission announcements); born on or after 1 July 2006; a separate Overseas merit list is prepared.
  • NRI with an Indian passport: not eligible for the Overseas category — apply as an Indian national (general fee, general age limit).
  • OCI / PIO: check the nationality clause (Section 5.2) of the handbook for your case before choosing a category.
  • Documents: a valid passport (first and last pages, JPG) is mandatory for Overseas applicants; the passport must not expire during the process. A student visa is required before joining.
