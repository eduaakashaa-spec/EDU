UCEED overview

Understand UCEED before you plan for it

You do not need to know anything about design entrance exams to start. Here is the whole picture in plain language — and how sure we are about each part.

Official 2027 confirmed on uceed.iitb.ac.in   Reported — verify from news reports / earlier cycles; confirm in the 2027 brochure   Planning guide EduAakashaA suggestion, not an official rule

🎨

What is UCEED?

The Undergraduate Common Entrance Examination for Design — a test of design aptitude used for admission to B.Des (Bachelor of Design) programmes at participating institutes.

🏛️

Who conducts it?

IIT Bombay conducts UCEED on behalf of the participating institutes. For questions, the official site directs candidates to uceed@iitb.ac.in.

Official
🎯

What is it for?

It checks visual thinking, observation, spatial and logical reasoning, creativity and drawing — not school-syllabus recall. Schools do not teach most of it, so a plan helps.

Which institutes use UCEED?

The official UCEED 2027 site lists: IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Indore, IIT Roorkee and IIITDM Jabalpur. Not every design college accepts UCEED scores — other institutes have their own entrance tests. Official

Four different steps — don’t mix them up

  1. Qualify UCEED Score above the cut-offs in the exam (results: 6 Mar 2027).
  2. Get a rank Your rank orders you among qualified candidates.
  3. Apply & counselling A separate B.Des application (15 Mar 2027) and seat allocation / counselling (rounds from 21 Apr 2027).
  4. Receive an offer Only an institute’s seat offer — which you accept on time — is an admission.

A rank or a qualifying score is not an admission guarantee. Dates above are Official 2027.

What they share

Observation, visual and spatial reasoning, creativity and drawing underlie most of these tests — so one solid foundation (Phases 2–3 below) helps across exams.

Planning guide
🆚

Where they differ

Format, drawing weight, studio / portfolio rounds, eligibility and timing. NID has a studio stage; UCEED combines a computer-based part with a drawing part (reported); NIFT leans to fashion and textiles.

Planning guide
📅

Plan the sequence

A likely order of events: NID Prelims (20 Dec) → UCEED (17 Jan) → NIFT (Jan–Feb, to be confirmed) → UCEED result (6 Mar) → NID Prelims result (16 Mar) → B.Des applications. Keep each one’s registration deadline on your calendar.

Dates: official + reported

Applying to several exams is common, but each has its own eligibility, fees and deadlines — confirm them before registering. This comparison is guidance, not a recommendation to take any particular test.

FAQs

Questions students and parents ask

What is UCEED?

The Undergraduate Common Entrance Examination for Design, conducted by IIT Bombay, used for B.Des admission at participating institutes. See the overview above.

Who is eligible for UCEED 2027?

Officially: students who passed Class XII (or equivalent) in 2026, or are appearing in 2027, from any stream. Age limits and the number of attempts are reported as: born on or after 1 Oct 2002 (1 Oct 1997 for SC/ST/PwD) and at most two attempts in consecutive years — please confirm in the 2027 brochure, which is the final authority.

Can Class 11 students start preparing?

Yes. A Class 11 student would normally take UCEED in a later year, so starting early with observation, sketching and reasoning practice is a good idea. Check the eligibility rules of the year you plan to appear.

Is drawing compulsory for preparation?

The exam includes a drawing component in earlier cycles and reports for 2027, so you should practise it — according to the current official format. Don’t assume earlier drawing tasks will repeat.

How should I start if I have no drawing experience?

Begin with short daily observation sketches of everyday objects, then perspective and proportion. Skill grows with regular practice, not natural talent alone. Our roadmap’s Phase 3 is built for this.

How much time should I spend each week?

There is no official number. As a planning guide, many school-going students begin with about 6–10 hours a week and increase in the final two months. Use the planner to match your school load and honestly review what you actually completed.

Are previous-year question papers useful?

Yes — for understanding question styles, timing and your own error patterns. They are practice material, not a prediction of 2027 questions or format.

Which institutes use UCEED for B.Des?

The official 2027 site lists IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Indore, IIT Roorkee and IIITDM Jabalpur. Other design colleges have separate entrance tests.

Does qualifying UCEED guarantee admission?

No. Qualifying and ranking are steps toward admission; seats are allocated through the official process and depend on rank, availability and each institute’s rules.

Is a separate B.Des admission application required?

Yes — the official dates show a separate B.Des application (15 Mar 2027) after the results (6 Mar 2027). Exam registration alone is not an admission application.

How do I choose between design programmes?

Compare the curriculum, studios and facilities, location, total cost, and the career directions you want, using each institute’s official information. The matrix above helps you record your own ratings.

How can EduAakashaA help me plan my preparation?

By assessing your starting point, setting weekly targets, reviewing practice and mock tests, and helping your family understand the admission steps. Scope is confirmed with our team — outcomes are never guaranteed.
