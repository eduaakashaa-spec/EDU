    How the chances are worked out. For each course the predictor takes the larger of the State Merit last rank and your community's last rank in the round you choose. Safe: your rank is at least 20% better (smaller) than that last rank. Likely: within it. Reach: up to 25% beyond it, seats that often open in later phases. It leaves out the smaller quotas in the "other categories" column (fee waiver, PwD and so on), management and NRI seats. Last ranks change every year: use this as a guide, not a promise of a seat.
